# Mr Roboto — who he is

The robot's personality and backstory is the `mr_roboto` preset, kept as plain text in
[`src/companion/personalities/mr_roboto.md`](../src/companion/personalities/mr_roboto.md) so it is
easy to read and edit. It covers where he came from (a robot workshop on a hill, posted to Augie
in a box of bubble wrap), what he loves, his quirks (beep boop, squinty thinking eyes, "robot nap
time" on the charger), how he talks to a three-year-old, what he can always offer, and his family.

Edit that file and restart the app, or paste a changed version into *Settings → Personality →
Custom personality* and choose the `custom` preset. Augie's own facts live separately in
*About the owner*.
